Features

Everything the remote does,
and a lot it can't.

Click the icon for the control center. Right-click it for the full menu. Or never touch the mouse at all.

Control center

What's playing with a progress bar, volume, your apps with one click, scenes and quick actions, right under the menu bar icon.

Floating remote

A glass remote that floats over your work. Swipe to move, click for OK, drag to scrub. Collapses to a small bar when you don't need it.

Phone remote

Scan a QR code and your phone becomes the remote. No app to install. Add it to the Home Screen and it feels like one.

Global hotkeys

Play, pause, volume and the remote from any app with ⌃⌥ shortcuts.

Cast from your Mac

Send a video file, a link or your current browser tab to the TV. YouTube and Netflix links open straight in their apps.

Scenes and sleep timer

"Movie night" sets the volume and opens Netflix in one step. Fall asleep to a show and the TV turns itself off.

Voice and typing

Hold V to search with your voice through the Mac's microphone. Press T and type on the TV with a real keyboard.

Command line

A beambar command for Shortcuts, Siri, Raycast and Stream Deck. Anything that runs a shell command can drive the TV.

More than one TV

Finds the TVs on your network by itself. Switch between the living room and the bedroom from the device menu.

Phone remote

Your phone is already
the remote.

Open the QR code from the control center and point your camera at it. The remote opens in the browser, with nothing to install.

  • Same remote everywhere. The phone gets the touch pad, media controls, apps and scenes, just like the Mac.
  • Type with the phone keyboard instead of picking letters one by one on the TV.
  • Live in both places. The QR window says "Phone connected" the moment your phone joins.
  • Stays on your Wi-Fi. The phone talks to your Mac, and your Mac talks to the TV. Nothing goes through the internet.
Made for the Mac

Small things,
done right.

BeamBar is built with the Mac's own windows, menus and notifications.

  • Copied a link? BeamBar offers to paste it when you open "Cast a link" or "Open on TV".
  • Mistakes are caught early. A broken link or a wrong pairing code is pointed out before anything is sent.
  • Everything in one place. The More page has casting, screen mirroring, hotkeys, start at login and pairing.
  • Quiet by default. It sits in the menu bar with the volume next to the icon, and stays out of the Dock.
Command line

Script your living room.

The beambar command talks to the running app, so Shortcuts, Siri, Raycast and Stream Deck can all control the TV.

  • "Hey Siri, movie night": a Shortcut with one shell step runs your scene.
  • Status as JSON for your own scripts and dashboards.
$ beambar volume 40
$ beambar app Netflix
$ beambar open stranger things   # YouTube search
$ beambar cast ~/Movies/trip.mp4
$ beambar scene "Movie night"
$ beambar sleep 30               # off in 30 minutes
$ beambar find                   # look for TVs
$ beambar status
Install

Up and running in two minutes.

BeamBar runs from source for now. It's tested on macOS 15 and needs Python 3.10 or newer (brew install python@3.12).

Download and install

The script sets everything up, adds the beambar command and starts the app.

git clone https://github.com/manoj6418/BeamBar.git
cd BeamBar
./install.sh

Turn on the TV

Make sure it's on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac. BeamBar finds it by itself. If you have more than one, pick it under Devices.

Pair once

Choose Pair with TV… and type the code the TV shows. You only do this once per TV.

Optional: hotkeys and start at login

Turn them on in the control center's More page. For hotkeys, allow BeamBar under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

📦A ready-to-run app is on the way. Until then, the install script above is the way to get BeamBar.
FAQ

Questions

Which TVs and projectors work?

Devices running Google TV or Android TV that support Google's remote control service, the same one the Google TV phone app uses. It's built and tested with an XGIMI projector. Other Google TV and Android TV devices should work too, but haven't been tested yet.

Does it need the internet?

No. BeamBar talks to the TV directly over your Wi-Fi. The phone remote is served by your Mac on the same network. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can it turn the TV on?

From standby, yes. From fully off, no: that needs the physical remote's Bluetooth wake signal, which a Mac can't send.

My phone can't open the link. What now?

Some phones can't open .local names. Switch the QR window to IP link and scan again. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac.

Is this an official Google or XGIMI app?

No. BeamBar is an independent project and isn't affiliated with Google or XGIMI.

Is there a download?

Not yet. A packaged app is coming. For now, install from source with the three commands above.
